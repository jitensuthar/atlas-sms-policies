How SMS consent works

Recipients may opt in through the public Atlas SMS Opt-In form. The form requests a name, provides an optional mobile-number field, and presents an optional SMS preference for non-marketing requested updates and coordination. Atlas sends no text unless a recipient both selects the affirmative SMS preference and provides a valid mobile number. The form displays links to these Terms and the Privacy Policy plus message/data-rate, STOP, and recognized HELP/INFO command information.

Alternatively, Jiten may obtain direct verbal permission to text a recipient about a specific purpose. Jiten records the recipient’s number, purpose, date, and source of permission before the first message is sent.

After valid consent from either path is recorded, Atlas’s first message identifies Atlas and Jiten, states the purpose, explains that message and data rates may apply, and gives STOP instructions. No reply or YES keyword is required after valid consent is recorded.

Atlas’s SMS program uses +1 (646) 713-1285. Atlas does not use marketing, promotional messages, purchased lists, or mass messaging. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Your choices

Reply STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, OPTOUT, or REVOKE to opt out. Reply HELP or INFO for the support route. After opting out, a recipient must give Jiten fresh direct permission for a specific purpose before Atlas initiates another text. Message and data rates may apply.
