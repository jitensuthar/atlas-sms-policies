Atlas SMS Privacy Policy

SMS ProgramTerms & Conditions

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This policy applies to SMS and MMS messages sent by Atlas, a software-based communications platform operated by Jiten Suthar.

Information Atlas handles

Atlas may process a recipient’s name, mobile phone number, message content, delivery information, consent selection, form-submission reference and time, and information voluntarily provided during a conversation. For Atlas-initiated messages, it records the recipient-specific consent basis, requested purpose, date, source of permission, and consent record needed to prevent unwanted messages.

How information is used

Information is used only to respond to a recipient, provide requested updates or coordination, operate the service, honor opt-out requests, prevent misuse, and meet legal obligations. Atlas does not use this program for marketing or promotional messages.

Sharing

Atlas uses Google Forms to collect public SMS opt-in submissions and Twilio to provide SMS and MMS. They process information only to provide those services. SMS opt-in consent and mobile numbers are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Atlas does not sell personal information.

Retention and security

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, honor consent choices, and meet legal obligations. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no communications system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and support

Reply STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, OPTOUT, or REVOKE to stop SMS messages. Reply HELP or INFO to receive the support route, then reply with your question for Jiten. Message and data rates may apply.

Your use of Atlas SMS is also governed by the Atlas SMS Terms & Conditions.
