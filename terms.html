Program description

Atlas sends and receives conversational SMS and MMS messages, replies, requested updates, appointment coordination, and follow-ups related to a recipient’s existing interaction or request. Atlas does not use this program for marketing or promotional messages. These terms do not govern phone calls.

Consent

Recipients may opt in through the public Atlas SMS Opt-In form by selecting the optional affirmative SMS preference for requested updates and coordination. A mobile number is optional on the form, and Atlas sends no text unless the recipient both selects the affirmative preference and provides a valid mobile number. The form also provides an explicit decline choice, and SMS consent is not required to use non-SMS contact options. The form displays message/data-rate, STOP, and recognized HELP/INFO command information plus links to these Terms and the Privacy Policy. Atlas records the form-submission reference and time before sending the one-time confirmation message.

Alternatively, Jiten may obtain direct verbal permission to send texts about a specific purpose. Jiten records the recipient’s number, consent basis, purpose, date, and source of permission before the first message is sent. For either consent path, the first message identifies Atlas and Jiten, states the purpose, and includes message/data-rate and STOP disclosures. The recipient does not need to send YES or any reply after valid consent is recorded.

Consent is not a condition of purchase. Atlas does not use web-form signups for marketing, purchased lists, or mass messaging.

Message cost

Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Opt out and support

Reply STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, OPTOUT, or REVOKE to cancel SMS messages at any time. Reply HELP or INFO to receive the support route, then reply with a support question for Jiten. After opting out, Atlas will not initiate further messages unless the recipient gives Jiten fresh direct permission for a specific purpose and Jiten records it before another Atlas-initiated text.
